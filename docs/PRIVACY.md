# Privacy: bug reports and feedback

Last Ink does not track you. It has no analytics, no accounts and no ads, and it never sends
anything over the internet by itself.

## What stays on your device

- **Logs.** The game writes a log of each session to its log folder: the game version, your
  operating system, processor, graphics card, screen size and language, which scene and mission you
  were in, which save slot you used, and any errors. The last five sessions are kept; older ones are
  deleted. On the web version, the log is stored in your browser like your saves.
- **Screenshots** you take with F12 go to the game's screenshot folder (the web version downloads them).

"Log folder" in the report form (and "Open log folder" on the title screen after a crash) takes you
to the logs; the screenshot folder sits next to it. The web version downloads the logs instead.

## What a report sends, and only when you press Send

"Report a bug" (title screen and every pause menu) opens a form. Nothing leaves your device unless
you press **Send**. A report contains:

- what you type, and the kind you pick (bug, feedback or idea);
- a way to reach you, only if you fill it in;
- the attachments you leave ticked: a screenshot of the game as it was when you opened the form,
  the logs described above, and your current save file;
- the game version and system information listed above, and where you were in the game.

It does **not** contain your name, account, files other than those listed, or your IP address.
The report server sees your IP address only to stop spam (a few reports per hour per address) and
does not store it.

"Save to file" writes the same report as a zip on your device instead, so you can read it and share
it any way you like.

## Where reports go and how long they are kept

Reports are stored in a private GitHub repository that only the developers can read. Bugs found in
them are copied into the public issue tracker as a description of the problem only, without what
you wrote word for word, your contact details or your save. Reports are deleted once the bug they
describe is fixed and released, and at the latest after 12 months.

To have a report deleted earlier, or to ask what a report held, write to the developers on the
game's page and quote the report number the game showed you after sending (it looks like
`LI-1A2B3C4D`).
